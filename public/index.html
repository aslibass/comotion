Good help already exists — from Arthritis Australia, the NSW Pain Network, painHEALTH, the Pain Toolkit, Painaustralia and others. But it's hard to find at 2am with a joint on fire. Co-Motion brings it together: ask a question in your own words, get an answer from those services, and find out what to do tonight.

Every answer tells you what to do next

You won't just get information. You'll be told what to do — and most of the time, that you can safely look after it where you are. Every answer ends with one of five steps.

  • You can do this at homeMost questions end here.
  • Your pharmacist can help todayNo appointment. Talking to them costs nothing.
  • This one's for your GP, and it can waitWe write it down for you, with the date, so you can take it to your next visit.
  • Call your GP todayOr call healthdirect — a nurse answers, any hour.
  • Get seen tonightThis doesn't happen often. When it does, we tell you straight away.

Two promises we keep

We check for danger first

A hot, swollen joint with a fever can be an infected joint — and it can look just like a gout flare you've had many times before. Before you get any answer, we ask you a few quick questions about signs like this. If any of them apply, we tell you to get help straight away. That check is fixed and always works, even if the rest of the page doesn't.

We don't make up health advice

What we explain comes only from the Australian health services listed below. If they don't cover your question, we tell you so — and we save the question for you to ask your doctor, instead of guessing.
